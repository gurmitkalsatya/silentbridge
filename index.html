SilentBridge System Architecture

SilentBridge is an emergency offline acoustic peer-to-peer mesh communicator designed for extreme disaster scenarios where cell towers, Wi-Fi, internet, and satellite uplinks are down.

// ACOUSTIC SPECIFICATION & VOICE TELEMETRY
• Carrier Band: 18.0 - 19.5 kHz (Near-Ultrasonic) / 1.5 - 2.2 kHz (Demo)
• Packet Schema: 32-Byte Fixed ArrayBuffer + CCITT CRC-16 Checksum
• High-Precision GPS: IEEE-754 Float32 with ±Accuracy Radius
• Voice Dispatch: Emergency Voice Recording + Waveform Audio Playback
• Autonomous Mesh: Multi-hop relay with randomized jitter backoff
